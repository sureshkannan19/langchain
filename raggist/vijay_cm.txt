Vijay as Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu: Facts and Events

Overview

C. Joseph Vijay, popularly known as Thalapathy Vijay, was sworn in as the 22nd Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu on May 10, 2026. His party Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), founded just two years earlier in February 2024, emerged as the single largest party in the 2026 Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly election, winning 108 of the 234 seats. This historic victory ended nearly six decades of alternating dominance between the DMK and AIADMK in Tamil Nadu politics.

Formation of Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam

On February 2, 2024, actor Vijay announced the formation of his political party, Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), stating that it would contest the 2026 elections. The party name translates to Tamil Victory Party in English. TVK positioned itself as an ideological opponent of both the Bharatiya Janata Party and the established Dravidian parties, DMK and AIADMK. Vijay categorically rejected any possibility of joining hands with what he called ideological enemies, particularly the BJP.

In a special general council meeting held at Mahabalipuram near Chennai, TVK officially declared Vijay as its Chief Ministerial candidate for the 2026 Tamil Nadu Assembly elections. The council adopted twelve resolutions addressing key state and party issues, including authorizing Vijay to make all decisions regarding 2026 electoral alliances.

2026 Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly Election

Elections to appoint the 234 members of the 17th Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly were held on April 23, 2026. The results were declared on May 4, 2026 by the Election Commission of India. The election recorded the highest voter turnout in Tamil Nadu state history at 85.1 percent.

On March 18, 2026, Vijay announced that the TVK would contest solo in all 234 constituencies, and released the complete candidate list on March 30, 2026. Though TVK announced candidates for all 234 constituencies, the nomination of the official TVK candidate was rejected in the Edappadi Assembly constituency. The party later extended support to K. Prem Kumar, who contested as an Independent candidate.

Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam won 108 of the 234 seats, emerging as the single largest party in its very first election. The party ended a 59-year streak of dominance of Dravidian parties in Tamil Nadu. Analysts and media commentators noted that the party's campaign relied heavily on digital outreach and social media platforms including YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp. BBC described the campaign as reflecting a major shift towards digital-first political mobilisation in India.

The incumbent DMK led the Secular Progressive Alliance, which remained in opposition. Tamil Nadu had recorded strong economic and social indicators under the previous DMK government, including 11.2 percent economic growth in 2024 to 2025 and continued manufacturing expansion.

Government Formation

After the election results, TVK secured the support of 120 MLAs, crossing the majority mark of 118 in the 234-member assembly. TVK won 108 seats and allied with the Indian National Congress which won five seats. The Communist Party of India, Communist Party of India (Marxist), Viduthalai Chiruthaigal Katchi, and Indian Union Muslim League, which won two seats each, extended unconditional support from outside while remaining part of the SPA alliance led by the DMK.

Governor Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar approved the list of ministers recommended by Vijay for induction into the new cabinet.

Swearing-In Ceremony

C. Joseph Vijay was sworn in as the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu on Sunday, May 10, 2026, at the Jawaharlal Nehru Indoor Stadium in Chennai in the presence of several national and regional leaders. Governor Rajendra Arlekar administered the oath of office and secrecy to Vijay and his council of ministers. This ended almost six decades of alternating rule by Tamil Nadu's two dominant Dravidian parties, DMK and AIADMK.

Vijay became the 22nd Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu and the 13th Chief Minister since the linguistic reformation of the state's boundaries in 1956.

Cabinet Ministers

Vijay was sworn in alongside a nine-member council of ministers. The ministers who took oath along with Vijay are:

N. Anand, widely regarded as Vijay's closest aide at 61 years of age and described as the leader most trusted by the Chief Minister.

Aadhav Arjuna, a cabinet minister in the new government.

Dr K.G. Arunraj, a cabinet minister in the new government.

K.A. Sengottaiyan, a veteran politician who served as Chief Coordinator of the High-level Administrative Committee of TVK from November 27, 2025, and previously served as Cabinet Minister in the Government of Tamil Nadu between 2017 and 2021 holding the School Education and Youth Welfare portfolios under Chief Minister Edappadi K. P. Venkataramanan, who won the Mylapore constituency on a TVK ticket with 70,070 votes. A postgraduate by qualification, he declared no criminal cases in his election affidavit. R. Nirmalkumar, a cabinet minister in the new government.

Dr T.K. Prabhu, a dentist by profession who contested the Tamil Nadu assembly election 2026 from Karaikudi constituency in Sivaganga. His educational qualification is postgraduate.

S. Keerthana, the only woman minister in Vijay's initial cabinet. As per her election affidavit, she has total assets of Rs 22.6 lakh and liabilities of Rs 12.8 lakh, and declared an annual income of Rs 6.4 lakh with no pending criminal cases.

TVK Party Background and Campaign

TVK was a two-year-old party at the time of the 2026 election, having been founded in February 2024. Despite its youth, it managed to contest alone in 233 constituencies and win 108 seats. The party's campaign was distinct from traditional Tamil Nadu political campaigns that had relied on public rallies, speeches, banners, door-to-door canvassing, and extensive media outreach. TVK's digital-first approach through social media platforms marked a significant shift in how political campaigns were conducted in the state.

The party positioned itself as a rival to the oscillating duopoly of the DMK and AIADMK that had dominated Tamil Nadu politics for nearly six decades. TVK also positioned itself as an ideological opponent of the BJP, the ruling party of the Government of India.

Historical Significance

The 2026 Tamil Nadu election result was historically significant for multiple reasons. TVK became the single largest party in its very first election, an achievement rarely seen in Indian political history. The election ended a 59-year streak of Dravidian party dominance in Tamil Nadu. The voter turnout of 85.1 percent was the highest ever recorded in the state's election history. Vijay became the first Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu who entered politics from the film industry and led his own party to victory on his first electoral attempt.
